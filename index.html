06 — About SNF

A trusted partner from concept to completion.

SNF Construction is a full-service general contractor serving clients throughout California. We handle residential, commercial, industrial, and multifamily work — custom homes and ADUs, office and retail buildouts, restaurants, medical suites, warehouses, and large-scale developments.

What sets a job apart is rarely the materials. It is the crew, the schedule, and whether someone picks up the phone when you call. We build around those three things: quality craftsmanship, transparent communication, and a commitment to hitting the dates and the numbers we agreed to.
